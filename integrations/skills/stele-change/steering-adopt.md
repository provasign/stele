To adopt a project's existing specs (the registry has no rules yet, or a Stele message says so), run `stele adopt --json` and follow it exactly as `stele change` above, with `adopt` as the change id; it asks the owner only what needs a person, then for the approval. For a large project, `stele adopt --list` shows its specs and `stele adopt --json --spec <name>[,<name>]` adopts some of them first; the summary names the specs not read yet.
